CEO Insider View

When a company's own CEO buys its stock with their own money, it's public within 2 business days. Every buy here comes straight from the SEC filing and links back to it.

How this works, and what the labels mean

Where the data comes from. Every buy is read from the CEO's SEC Form 4. Only open-market purchases count (transaction code P). Free shares, option exercises and tax sales are left out. Same-day purchases are added up at the average price.

How fast. This page checks the SEC every 30 seconds. The law gives insiders 2 business days to file, so a buy can be up to 2 days old when it appears. Nobody legally sees it sooner.

Why there's no score. We used to rank buys with a 0 to 100 score. We tested it on 2,116 CEO buys from 2025 and 2026, and buys that scored high did worse afterwards, so we removed it. Each buy now shows the facts from its filing, marked ✓ or ! where our test found they went with better or worse results. Read the test
